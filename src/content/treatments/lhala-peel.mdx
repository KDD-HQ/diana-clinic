## Why LHALA Peel is used for glow, pores and texture

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>LHALA Peel is often chosen for patients who want brighter-looking skin, smoother texture and clearer-looking pores with minimal downtime.</strong> It may be considered for dullness, mild congestion, oily skin tendency, rough surface texture and uneven-looking skin tone.</p>
</div>

Unlike stronger peel treatments that may cause more visible peeling or longer recovery, LHALA Peel is positioned as a gentler skin renewal option. It may be suitable for people who want a glow-focused treatment before events or as regular skin maintenance, provided their skin is not actively irritated, inflamed or barrier-damaged.

At Klinik Dr Diana, LHALA Peel is not treated as a one-size-fits-all facial. Your skin condition, sensitivity, acne activity, pigmentation tendency and aesthetic goals are checked first before the treatment is recommended.

## What can LHALA Peel help with?

## How does LHALA Peel work?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>LHALA Peel works by using an LHA-based peeling approach with skin-supporting lipids to gently soften and exfoliate dead skin buildup while supporting smoother texture, clearer-looking pores and overall glow.</strong> In clinic practice, the treatment usually includes skin preparation, LHALA Peel application, and soothing or moisturising finishing care.</p>
</div>

LHA, also known as lipohydroxy acid, is related to salicylic acid and is commonly used in skincare for gentle exfoliation and clogged-pore support. LHALA Peel is often described as a newer-generation Korean peel because it combines LHA with lipid-based or barrier-supporting components.

The goal is gentle skin renewal rather than aggressive surface stripping. Although LHALA Peel is designed to be gentler than many traditional chemical peels, temporary redness, tingling, dryness, tightness or sensitivity may still happen depending on skin condition.

## What to expect during the session

A LHALA Peel session begins with skin consultation and cleansing. The peel is then applied according to your skin condition and tolerance, followed by soothing care, hydration support and aftercare advice.

Most patients may feel mild tingling, warmth or temporary sensitivity during or after the peel. Downtime is usually minimal, but mild redness, dryness or tightness can happen. Moisturiser and sunscreen are important after treatment.
