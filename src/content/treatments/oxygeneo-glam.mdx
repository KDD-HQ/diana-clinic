## How does OxyGeneo Glam work?

<div class="answer">
<p>OxyGeneo Glam works in <strong>three sequential stages</strong>: exfoliation, oxygenation, and active infusion. The signature mechanism is CO₂-induced oxygenation, which triggers the Bohr effect — a physiological response where elevated local CO₂ causes hemoglobin to release more oxygen into the treated tissue.</p>
</div>

### 1. Exfoliation

<figure class="fig fig-step">
<img src="/images/treatments/oxygeneo-glam/glam-step1-exfoliation.jpg" alt="Illustration of OxyGeneo exfoliation step showing the OxyPod reacting with Primer Gel on the skin surface" width="800" height="900" loading="lazy" />
<figcaption>Step 1 — Gentle, non-abrasive exfoliation via OxyPod + Primer Gel reaction.</figcaption>
</figure>

An OxyGeneo OxyPod reacts with a Primer Gel applied to the skin, gently dissolving the stratum corneum (outer dead skin layer). This is non-abrasive — unlike microdermabrasion — and prepares the skin to absorb actives more efficiently.

### 2. CO₂ Oxygenation (Bohr Effect)

<figure class="fig fig-step">
<img src="/images/treatments/oxygeneo-glam/glam-step2-oxygenation.jpg" alt="Illustration of CO2-induced oxygenation showing oxygen bubbles being triggered inside the skin layers" width="800" height="900" loading="lazy" />
<figcaption>Step 2 — CO₂ at the skin surface triggers the Bohr effect, increasing internal oxygen delivery.</figcaption>
</figure>

The OxyPod-gel reaction generates a CO₂-rich bubbling layer at the skin surface. The body interprets this as a localised oxygen deficit and responds by directing oxygen-rich blood to the area. The result is improved microcirculation, enhanced cellular metabolism, and a visible flush of healthy radiance.

### 3. Active Infusion

<figure class="fig fig-step">
<img src="/images/treatments/oxygeneo-glam/glam-step3-nourishment.jpg" alt="Illustration of ultrasound-assisted infusion of serum and active ingredients into oxygenated skin" width="800" height="900" loading="lazy" />
<figcaption>Step 3 — Ultrasound-assisted infusion of Glam actives into the prepared, oxygenated skin.</figcaption>
</figure>

While the skin is primed and oxygenated, the device delivers ultrasound-assisted infusion of 24K gold flakes, Carnosine Peptide, Silk Amino Acids, Hyaluronic Acid, and PhytoSpherix™. Gold supports antioxidant and luminosity benefits; Carnosine Peptide signals collagen and elastin pathways; Silk Amino Acids and Hyaluronic Acid strengthen the skin barrier and improve hydration retention.

## What is inside the Glam OxyPod?

<div class="answer">
<p>The OxyGeneo Glam OxyPod contains <strong>five clinically active components</strong>: <strong>24K Gold Flakes</strong> for antioxidant and luminosity benefit, <strong>Carnosine Peptide</strong> to signal collagen and elastin pathways, <strong>Silk Amino Acids</strong> to repair the skin barrier, <strong>Hyaluronic Acid</strong> for deep hydration, and <strong>PhytoSpherix™</strong> for moisture retention and skin-glycoprotein support.</p>
</div>

<ul class="clean">
<li><strong>24K Gold Flakes</strong> — antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, enhances surface light reflection for radiance.</li>
<li><strong>Carnosine Peptide</strong> — a dipeptide that signals fibroblasts toward collagen and elastin synthesis; primary mechanism for the anti-aging benefit.</li>
<li><strong>Silk Amino Acids</strong> — small molecules that penetrate the stratum corneum to improve hydration retention and barrier strength.</li>
<li><strong>Hyaluronic Acid</strong> — humectant that binds water in the dermis; contributes to the immediate plumping and smoothing effect.</li>
<li><strong>PhytoSpherix™</strong> — plant-derived glycogen that supports skin hydration, energy metabolism, and barrier function.</li>
</ul>

## What to expect during the session

A full session at Klinik Dr Diana takes approximately 45 minutes. After cleansing, the OxyGeneo OxyPod and Primer Gel are applied — you'll feel gentle bubbling and a light warming sensation. The device is then moved over the face in circular motions during the oxygenation phase, followed by the active infusion. Most patients describe the experience as relaxing rather than uncomfortable.

There is no peeling, no scabbing, and no required downtime. Makeup can be applied immediately afterward. For maintenance, we typically recommend treatments every 3 to 4 weeks.
