## What is Novuma biostimulator treatment?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Novuma is an injectable calcium hydroxylapatite (CaHA) biostimulator that may support facial firmness, density and gradual collagen regeneration.</strong> Manufactured by Laboratoires Vivacy, Novuma uses smooth, round CaHA microspheres in a carboxymethyl cellulose gel carrier, and is CE-marked (CE 2292) and registered with the Medical Device Authority (MDA) Malaysia.</p>
</div>

Novuma is different from a simple dermal filler. It does not mainly work by adding volume. Instead, the CaHA microspheres act as a scaffold that signals your own fibroblasts to produce new collagen over time, while the gel carrier may provide an earlier lifting effect depending on how the product is placed.

At Klinik Dr Diana, the first step is not choosing the product name. The first step is checking whether your concern is firmness loss, skin density, facial contour, texture, hydration, acne scars, pigmentation or a combination of issues.

## What you need to know

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Novuma can be helpful when the goal matches what CaHA biostimulation can realistically do: restore firmness, improve skin density and stimulate gradual collagen regeneration.</strong> It is not suitable for every skin concern and should not be chosen without assessment.</p>
</div>

<ul class="clean">
<li>Novuma is made of calcium hydroxylapatite (CaHA) microspheres, concentrated at 55–60% in a carboxymethyl cellulose gel carrier.</li>
<li>It uses Vivacy's Advanced BioSphere Technology, with smooth, round particles designed for consistent biostimulation.</li>
<li>Some lifting effect may be seen early, while collagen-related changes develop gradually over months.</li>
<li>It is not a hydration booster and is not primarily for pigmentation or active acne.</li>
<li>It is not dissolved with hyaluronidase like many hyaluronic acid fillers.</li>
<li>Correct placement, patient selection and injector experience are important for safety.</li>
</ul>

## Why is Novuma called a next-generation biostimulator?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Novuma is described as a next-generation biostimulator because its CaHA microspheres are engineered to be smooth and round for consistent collagen stimulation with controlled inflammation.</strong> This mechanism may support firmness, elasticity and skin structure in suitable patients.</p>
</div>

This is different from a standard filler-only effect. A typical filler adds volume by occupying space. A biostimulator like Novuma also aims to improve the tissue environment gradually by encouraging fibroblasts to build new collagen.

The word "stimulator" should still be understood carefully. It describes the mechanism, not a guarantee. Results vary depending on skin quality, age, placement, technique and biological response.

## How does Novuma work?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Novuma works through a dual mechanism: an early lifting effect from the gel carrier and a gradual biostimulatory effect from CaHA microspheres.</strong> Over time, the microspheres are gradually broken down and absorbed by the body, leaving behind a newly built collagen network that continues to support the skin.</p>
</div>

How Novuma behaves depends on technique. When placed structurally, it may support contour and lift. Across a broader treatment plan, it is used to encourage collagen remodelling for firmness and skin quality.

## Who may be suitable for Novuma?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Novuma may suit selected adults who are experiencing loss of firmness, softened facial contour or reduced skin density, and who want a gradual, collagen-supporting approach rather than volume-only treatment.</strong> It is not a one-size-fits-all injectable.</p>
</div>

Novuma may be considered for patients who understand that the treatment must be planned according to anatomy and that collagen-related changes take time. Patients who want hydration only, very soft lip enhancement, tear-trough correction, active acne care or pigmentation treatment may need different options.

## Who may not be suitable?

Novuma may not be suitable for patients who are pregnant or breastfeeding, have active infection or inflammation in the treatment area, have certain allergies or medical conditions, or have a treatment goal that would be safer or more appropriate with another product.

It may also be unsuitable when a patient wants a reversible hyaluronic acid filler, a hydration booster, or a treatment for concerns Novuma does not primarily address.

## What results can you realistically expect?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Novuma results depend on how it is used.</strong> Structural use may show an earlier lifting effect, while collagen-related firmness and skin-quality benefits develop gradually over weeks to months.</p>
</div>

Results are not permanent and cannot be guaranteed. Maintenance depends on the treated area, amount used, technique, metabolism, lifestyle and how your tissue responds.

## What are the possible side effects?

Common short-term effects may include swelling, bruising, redness, tenderness, itching, injection marks or temporary firmness. Lumps or nodules can occur, especially if product placement, technique or aftercare is not suitable.

Rare but serious biostimulator-related complications can include infection, vascular compromise, skin injury, scarring or vision-related complications. Seek urgent medical attention if you develop severe pain, skin colour changes, blistering, worsening swelling, visual symptoms or symptoms that feel unusual after injection.
