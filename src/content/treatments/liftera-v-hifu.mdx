## What is Liftera-V HIFU?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Liftera-V HIFU is a non-surgical lifting and skin-firming treatment that delivers focused ultrasound energy beneath the skin.</strong> It is intended to stimulate gradual collagen remodelling and may provide subtle tightening and improved facial definition in selected patients with mild to moderate laxity.</p>
</div>

Liftera-V uses a pen-style applicator designed to follow curved and smaller facial areas. Treatment depth, energy and coverage must be selected according to facial anatomy and the concern being treated.

At Klinik Dr Diana, Liftera-V is considered only after assessing whether your concern is mainly caused by skin laxity, facial fat, volume loss, muscle activity, bone structure or a combination. HIFU is not automatically the right treatment for every sagging face or double chin.

## What you need to know

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Liftera-V HIFU is best understood as a gradual firming treatment, not an instant or surgical facelift.</strong> Published evidence on microfocused ultrasound supports its use mainly for mild to moderate facial laxity, with outcomes that are generally modest and variable between patients [1].</p>
</div>

<ul class="clean">
<li>It uses focused ultrasound energy below the skin surface.</li>

<li>It cannot replace lost facial volume or correct every type of double chin.</li>

## Why is Liftera-V used as a lifting treatment?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Liftera-V is primarily used to support gradual firming and subtle lifting without incisions.</strong> It may help suitable patients who notice early lower-face softness, reduced jawline definition or mild laxity around the face and neck.</p>
</div>

Focused ultrasound creates controlled thermal points at selected depths beneath the skin. This initiates a tissue-repair response and collagen remodelling over time. Because the effect depends on the body's response, improvement is gradual and varies between individuals.

The realistic goal is modest tightening and improved firmness. Liftera-V does not physically remove loose skin or reposition deeper facial tissues like surgery.

## Why does the face begin to look less lifted with age?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Facial ageing is not caused by collagen loss alone.</strong> Skin laxity, changes in facial fat compartments, volume loss, muscle activity and gradual changes in bone support can all affect facial contours.</p>
</div>

This distinction matters because HIFU mainly targets tissue firmness and laxity. It cannot replace lost cheek volume, change the shape of the jawbone or fully correct heavy tissue descent. If several ageing changes are present at the same time, the doctor may discuss a different treatment or a carefully planned combination rather than relying on HIFU alone.

## How does Liftera-V HIFU work?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Liftera-V concentrates ultrasound energy at selected depths beneath the skin, creating controlled thermal treatment points while leaving the surface largely undisturbed.</strong> The resulting repair response may support new collagen formation and gradual tissue firming.</p>
</div>

The pen-style applicator is designed to move along facial curves and reach smaller or more contoured areas. This does not make treatment risk-free: correct depth, energy, anatomical knowledge and patient selection remain essential.

## Who may be suitable for Liftera-V HIFU?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Liftera-V may suit adults with mild to moderate skin laxity who want gradual, modest firming without surgery and who have realistic expectations.</strong> Suitability depends on anatomy and skin condition rather than age alone.</p>
</div>

It may be considered when the concern is early lower-face softness, mild jowling or reduced jawline definition related mainly to laxity. Patients expecting a dramatic or immediate facelift-level result are unlikely to be satisfied.

## Who may not be suitable?

Liftera-V may be unsuitable or require additional assessment for patients with significant loose skin, heavy jowls, active infection or inflammation, open wounds, or certain medical conditions and implanted devices. Pregnancy and previous facial procedures should be disclosed during consultation.

HIFU may also be the wrong treatment when the main issue is facial volume loss, a fat-dominant double chin, prominent muscle activity, pigmentation or surface texture. Another treatment or a combined plan may be more appropriate.

## What results can you realistically expect?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Expected Liftera-V results are gradual and generally subtle rather than dramatic.</strong> Suitable patients may notice firmer-feeling skin or slightly improved facial definition as collagen remodelling develops over several weeks to a few months.</p>
</div>

Results vary according to baseline laxity, age, skin quality, anatomy, treatment coverage and individual biological response. Some patients notice more change than others, and no responsible clinic can guarantee a fixed percentage of lifting or a precise duration.

## Can Liftera-V HIFU define the jawline or treat a double chin?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Liftera-V may improve jawline appearance when mild skin laxity is contributing to reduced definition, but it does not change bone structure and should not be promised as reliable double-chin fat removal.</strong></p>
</div>

Fullness beneath the chin can be caused by fat, loose skin, muscle position, salivary glands or the shape of the chin and jaw. A facial assessment is needed to identify the cause before choosing HIFU, fat-focused treatment, filler, surgery or another option.

## Is Liftera-V painful, and is there downtime?

Patients may feel brief warmth, tingling or discomfort while energy is delivered. Sensitivity varies, particularly around bony areas. Liftera-V should be described as potentially more comfortable for some patients, not painless.

Downtime is usually minimal, but short-term redness, swelling, tenderness, bruising, tingling or numbness may occur. Contact the clinic promptly if you experience severe or worsening pain, blistering, skin injury, weakness, drooping or symptoms that do not settle as advised.

## How many Liftera-V sessions are needed?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>There is no responsible fixed session schedule for every patient.</strong> The treatment plan depends on the degree of laxity, treatment area, previous procedures, response and clinical reassessment.</p>
</div>

Repeating HIFU too frequently or treating unsuitable areas may increase risk without improving the result. Klinik Dr Diana will advise whether maintenance is appropriate after reviewing your response.
