## What is Plenhyage Strong XL?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Plenhyage Strong XL is an injectable polynucleotide skin booster designed to support skin repair, hydration, firmness, elasticity and overall skin quality.</strong> It is commonly considered for patients with dull, tired-looking, dehydrated or ageing skin who want gradual skin improvement rather than a filler-like contouring effect.</p>
</div>

Plenhyage Strong XL belongs to the polynucleotide treatment category. Polynucleotides are used in aesthetic medicine to support skin repair, hydration and tissue quality. Unlike a traditional dermal filler, the goal is not to create sharp projection or obvious facial volume. The focus is on skin quality, recovery support and gradual rejuvenation.

At Klinik Dr Diana, Plenhyage Strong XL is not treated as a one-size-fits-all injection. Your skin quality, hydration level, fine lines, acne activity, pigmentation tendency, sensitivity, previous treatments and healing response are assessed first before treatment is recommended.

## How does Plenhyage Strong XL work?

<div class="answer">
