## What is Hyalual Electri?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Hyalual Electri is an injectable skin booster or redermalisation treatment that combines hyaluronic acid with succinic acid.</strong> It is commonly used to support skin hydration, glow, smoother-looking texture, fine-line appearance and selected delicate under-eye skin-quality concerns.</p>
</div>

Hyalual Electri is not a facial, peel, laser or structural filler. It is an injectable treatment placed into the skin through small injection points. The goal is to improve skin quality rather than reshape the face.

At Klinik Dr Diana, Electri is considered only after checking your concern properly. Dullness, dark eye circles, uneven tone and tired-looking skin can have different causes, so the treatment should be matched to the diagnosis rather than chosen by trend.

## Why does Hyalual Electri combine hyaluronic acid and succinic acid?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Hyaluronic acid helps bind water in the skin, while succinic acid is used for its antioxidant and skin-revitalising role.</strong> Together, they are intended to support hydration, skin recovery and a fresher-looking complexion.</p>
</div>

Hyaluronic acid is widely used in aesthetic medicine because it helps improve water balance and skin plumpness. Succinic acid is included in Electri as part of its redermalisation concept, with the aim of supporting skin metabolism and reducing oxidative stress.

This does not mean every patient will see the same result. Skin quality, sleep, sun exposure, inflammation, pigmentation tendency and under-eye anatomy all affect what treatment can realistically achieve.

</div>

The injection approach may involve small papules or microinjection points. This is why temporary bumps, redness or bruising can happen after treatment.

## Is Hyalual Electri good for dark eye circles?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Hyalual Electri may help some under-eye concerns when the problem is related to dehydration, fine lines or poor skin quality.</strong> It may not help much if dark circles are mainly caused by hollowness, veins, pigmentation, eye bags or facial anatomy.</p>
</div>

This is why dark eye circles should be assessed before treatment. Under-eye skin is delicate, and the wrong treatment can cause puffiness, unevenness or disappointment.

## Can Hyalual Electri help pigmentation or melasma?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Hyalual Electri should not be treated as a primary pigmentation or melasma treatment.</strong> It may support overall skin radiance, but dark patches need diagnosis first because melasma, PIH, sun spots and other pigmentation types behave differently.</p>
</div>

For Asian and pigmentation-prone skin, any injectable treatment can trigger inflammation, bruising or post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation in some patients. A careful plan is important.

## Who may be suitable for Hyalual Electri?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Hyalual Electri may suit selected adults who want skin hydration, glow, smoother-looking texture or under-eye skin-quality support without adding facial volume.</strong> Suitability depends on assessment.</p>
</div>

It may be considered for patients with dehydrated-looking skin, tired-looking skin, fine lines or dullness who understand that results are gradual and maintenance may be needed.

## Who may not be suitable?

Hyalual Electri may be unsuitable for patients who are pregnant or breastfeeding, have active infection or inflammation, significant under-eye swelling tendency, poorly controlled skin disease, certain allergies, or expectations that do not match what the treatment can realistically do.

It may also be the wrong choice if the main issue is deep tear-trough hollowing, significant eye bags, severe pigmentation, acne scars or facial volume loss.

## What results can you realistically expect?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Hyalual Electri results are usually about skin quality rather than facial reshaping.</strong> Suitable patients may notice fresher hydration, smoother texture, improved glow or softer-looking fine lines, but results vary.</p>
</div>

The manufacturer reports hydration improvement data after treatment, but individual results depend on skin condition, injection technique, aftercare and biological response. Avoid expecting a guaranteed percentage improvement or a permanent result.

## What are the possible side effects?

Common short-term effects include small injection bumps, redness, swelling, bruising, tenderness, itching or sensitivity. Under-eye treatment can sometimes cause temporary puffiness, especially in patients prone to fluid retention.

Less common concerns include infection, prolonged swelling, unevenness, allergic-type reaction or post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation in prone skin. Contact the clinic if swelling is severe, pain worsens, skin colour changes, or symptoms do not settle as advised.
