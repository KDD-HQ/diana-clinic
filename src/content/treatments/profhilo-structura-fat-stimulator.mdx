## What is Profhilo Structura?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Profhilo Structura is an injectable hyaluronic-acid-based bioremodelling treatment designed to support selected facial structure, tissue quality and skin firmness.</strong> It is commonly considered for patients who notice age-related facial softening, reduced cheek support, early sagging appearance or less defined facial contours.</p>
</div>

Profhilo Structura is different from a basic skin booster and different from a traditional filler. A normal skin booster usually focuses on hydration and skin glow. A traditional filler is often used to create shape, projection or volume. Profhilo Structura sits between these ideas because the goal is tissue support, firmness and natural-looking structural improvement rather than obvious reshaping.

At Klinik Dr Diana, Profhilo Structura is not recommended based on trend alone. The doctor first assesses whether your concern comes from skin laxity, facial fat change, volume loss, bone structure, muscle activity, pigmentation, texture or a combination of factors.

## Why is Profhilo Structura called a fat stimulator?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Profhilo Structura is sometimes called a fat stimulator because it is discussed for areas affected by age-related facial fat and structural softening.</strong> However, it should be explained carefully: it is not the same as surgical fat grafting, and it should not be promised as guaranteed fat replacement.</p>
</div>

As the face ages, some people notice flattening, hollowness, reduced cheek support or a softer lower-face appearance. This can happen because of changes in skin quality, facial fat compartments, collagen support, bone structure and overall tissue elasticity.

Profhilo Structura may be considered when the doctor feels the concern is related to tissue support and facial softening rather than only surface skin hydration. The treatment aim is usually subtle and gradual, with a natural-looking result rather than a visibly filled appearance.

## How does Profhilo Structura work?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Profhilo Structura works through an injectable bioremodelling approach that aims to support tissue quality and facial firmness.</strong> The exact treatment plan depends on facial assessment, injection area, ageing pattern and doctor judgement.</p>
</div>

The treatment is placed into selected areas using small injection points. The goal is not to overfill the face. Instead, the doctor assesses where support may be useful and whether Profhilo Structura matches the patient’s anatomy.

Because the intended effect is gradual tissue support, results may not look instantly dramatic. Some patients may notice early freshness or firmness, while structural-looking changes may take longer and vary between individuals.
