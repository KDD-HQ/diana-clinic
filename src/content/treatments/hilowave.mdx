## What is HILOWAVE?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>HILOWAVE is an injectable skin booster made from a dual hyaluronic acid complex, designed to support skin hydration, texture and firmness without the shaping effect of a filler.</strong> It is developed by HIGHER Corporation in South Korea and sold under the trademark HILO WAVE®.</p>
</div>

A skin booster is an injectable treatment placed within the skin to improve skin quality — hydration, smoothness and elasticity — rather than to change facial shape. HILOWAVE belongs to this group. The manufacturer also describes it as a collagen booster, because laboratory work suggests its hyaluronic acid complex may encourage skin cells to produce collagen.

You may see the product written as HILOWAVE, Hilowave or HILO WAVE. These are the same injectable product. If a treatment called HILO WAVE is described as needle-free, it is a different procedure from the HILOWAVE injectable used at Klinik Dr Diana.

## What you need to know about HILOWAVE

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>HILOWAVE is a doctor-performed skin-quality injectable with gradual, variable results and a still-developing evidence base.</strong> It suits people who want better hydration and texture, not people looking for lifting or new facial volume.</p>
</div>

<ul class="clean">
<li>HILOWAVE is an injectable treatment performed by a doctor. It is not a facial, a laser or a topical product.</li>
<li>HILOWAVE contains hyaluronic acid (HA) in two molecular sizes, combined into one product.</li>
<li>HILOWAVE is a skin-quality treatment, not a contouring filler. It is not designed to lift sagging skin or add defined volume.</li>
<li>Published research on HILOWAVE so far is laboratory and animal work. Human clinical trial data has not yet been published.</li>
<li>Results develop gradually and differ between individuals.</li>
<li>A consultation comes first, because dull or tired-looking skin can have several different causes.</li>

## What is inside HILOWAVE, and how is it designed to work?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>HILOWAVE contains three forms of hyaluronic acid: non-cross-linked low-molecular-weight HA, non-cross-linked high-molecular-weight HA, and a minimally cross-linked hybrid complex of the two.</strong> The combination is designed to hydrate the skin and support the skin's own repair activity, rather than to fill space.</p>
</div>

The key terms, in plain language:

<ul class="clean">
<li><strong>Hyaluronic acid (HA)</strong> is a sugar molecule found naturally in the skin. It binds water and helps keep skin hydrated and supple. Natural HA levels in the skin fall with age and sun damage.</li>
<li><strong>Fibroblasts</strong> are the skin cells that produce collagen and elastin, the proteins that give skin its firmness and spring.</li>
<li><strong>Low-molecular-weight HA</strong> is made of shorter HA chains. Shorter chains are thought to interact with fibroblasts and signal repair activity.</li>
<li><strong>High-molecular-weight HA</strong> is made of longer HA chains. Longer chains hold water and give the gel its body.</li>
<li><strong>Minimal cross-linking</strong> means a small amount of chemical bonding links the two HA sizes into one complex. HILOWAVE uses a trace amount of BDDE, the same type of cross-linker used in many HA fillers, but far less cross-linking than a shaping filler.</li>
</ul>

The idea behind HILOWAVE is that the two HA sizes work together. The larger HA hydrates, while the smaller HA and the hybrid complex may encourage the skin's own collagen production. Unlike some collagen stimulators that work by triggering a controlled inflammatory reaction, HILOWAVE is designed to support collagen with minimal inflammation. This proposed mechanism currently rests on laboratory and animal studies, not human trials.

## What does the research on HILOWAVE show so far?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>The main published study on HILOWAVE is a 2026 preclinical study — laboratory cell experiments and a rat model — published in the International Journal of Molecular Sciences.</strong> As of October 2026, we have not found a peer-reviewed human clinical trial of HILOWAVE.</p>
</div>

The 2026 study tested HILOWAVE (listed in the paper as DHC, a dual hyaluronic acid compound) on human skin cells in the laboratory and in UV-damaged rat skin. The authors describe their findings as showing biological potential rather than proven clinical results in people.

### Is HILOWAVE a filler?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>No. HILOWAVE is a skin booster, not a filler.</strong> A filler is a firmer, more cross-linked gel used to add defined volume or reshape areas such as the cheeks, chin or under-eye hollows. HILOWAVE is a fluid gel spread within the skin to improve skin quality.</p>
</div>

The difference matters because the two are chosen for different problems. Read <a href="/blog/skin-booster-vs-filler">skin booster vs filler</a> for a fuller explanation.

## Who may be suitable for HILOWAVE, and who should avoid it?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>HILOWAVE may suit healthy adults who want better skin hydration and texture with minimal downtime and who have realistic expectations.</strong> HILOWAVE should be avoided or postponed during pregnancy and breastfeeding, with active infection or inflammation in the treatment area, or with known allergy to hyaluronic acid.</p>
</div>

You may be suitable if:

<ul class="clean">
<li>Your main concern is dryness, dullness, rough texture or fine crepey lines</li>
<li>You want gradual, subtle change rather than a dramatic difference</li>
<li>You are comfortable with small injections and a few days of possible marks</li>
<li>You understand that HILOWAVE is a newer product with limited human data</li>
</ul>

HILOWAVE may need to be avoided or delayed if you:

<ul class="clean">
<li>Are pregnant or breastfeeding</li>
<li>Have an active skin infection, cold sores, an open wound or an acne flare in the treatment area</li>
<li>Have a known allergy or hypersensitivity to hyaluronic acid or any product component</li>
<li>Have a bleeding disorder or take blood-thinning medicine — this needs discussion first because bruising risk is higher</li>
<li>Have an active autoimmune condition or a recent serious illness</li>
<li>Recently had dental treatment, fillers, threads or laser in the same area — timing should be discussed with the doctor</li>
<li>Expect lifting, contouring or new facial volume</li>

## What happens during a HILOWAVE session at Klinik Dr Diana?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>A HILOWAVE session usually takes about 30 to 45 minutes, including numbing time.</strong> It involves a skin assessment, cleansing, numbing cream, small injections within the skin, and aftercare advice before you leave.</p>
</div>

### 1. Consultation and skin assessment

The doctor reviews your concern, medical history, medicines, current skincare, and any previous injectables or laser treatments. The doctor then checks skin hydration, texture, firmness and pigmentation. If HILOWAVE is not the right fit, the doctor will say so and explain the alternatives.

### 2. Treatment plan and consent

The treatment areas, amount of product and injection technique are planned. You are told the expected course, possible side effects and the cost before you agree to proceed.

### 3. Numbing and injection

Numbing cream is applied, then the skin is cleansed with antiseptic. HILOWAVE is injected in small amounts within the skin using a fine needle, a cannula or both, depending on the area. Most people feel brief stinging or pressure.

### 4. Aftercare and review

Small bumps at the injection points are expected and usually settle within a few days. You receive written aftercare advice and a review date if the doctor recommends one.

## When will you see results from HILOWAVE, and how long do they last?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Some people notice their skin looks more hydrated within one to two weeks of HILOWAVE, once swelling and bumps settle; changes in firmness or texture, if they occur, develop more gradually.</strong> There is no published human data yet on how long HILOWAVE results last, so any maintenance is planned after the doctor reviews your response.</p>
</div>

How your skin responds to HILOWAVE depends on your age, skin condition, sun exposure, smoking, skincare routine, hormonal changes and previous treatments. Results vary depending on skin type and severity of the concern, and individual assessment is required. HILOWAVE should not be expected to produce the effect of a filler, a lift or a resurfacing laser.

## How do the doctors at Klinik Dr Diana decide whether HILOWAVE is the right option?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>At Klinik Dr Diana, the doctor first identifies what is making the skin look tired — dehydration, texture, pigmentation, laxity or volume loss — and only then chooses a treatment.</strong> HILOWAVE is considered when the main problem is skin quality, and it may be combined with other treatments when more than one concern is present.</p>
</div>

The order of treatment matters. A typical decision path looks like this:

<ol>
<li><strong>Active acne is treated first.</strong> Injecting into inflamed skin raises the risk of infection.</li>
<li><strong>Pigmentation or melasma is brought under control with its own plan.</strong> A skin booster may be added later for skin quality.</li>
<li><strong>Acne scars need scar-specific treatment</strong>, such as subcision, TCA CROSS or fractional laser. A skin booster may support overall skin quality alongside it.</li>
<li><strong>Volume loss or sagging needs a structural option</strong>, such as a collagen biostimulator, HIFU or filler. A skin booster alone will not lift the face.</li>
<li><strong>When the main issue is dehydration, dullness or fine texture</strong>, HILOWAVE or another skin booster is considered.</li>
</ol>

Because HILOWAVE is newer, the doctor will also explain how its evidence compares with longer-established skin boosters such as <a href="/treatments/plenhyage-strong-xl">Plenhyage</a>, so you can choose with full information.
