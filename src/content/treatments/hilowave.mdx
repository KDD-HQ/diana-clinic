## What is HILOWAVE?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>HILOWAVE is an injectable hyaluronic acid (HA) biostimulator: it uses a dual HA complex designed to hydrate the skin and support the skin's own regeneration, rather than to fill or reshape the face.</strong> It is developed by HIGHER Corporation in South Korea and sold under the trademark HILO WAVE®.</p>
</div>

A biostimulator is an injectable treatment designed to encourage the skin to repair and renew itself. Many collagen stimulators, such as PLLA and PDLLA, do this through a controlled inflammatory response. HILOWAVE takes a different route: it uses hyaluronic acid in different molecular sizes to improve the skin's environment so that it favours regeneration, with minimal inflammation. The manufacturer describes it as a Dual-HA collagen booster.

Because HILOWAVE is placed within the skin and hydrates it, some clinics list it as a skin booster. HILOWAVE is designed to do more than hydrate, which is why Klinik Dr Diana groups it with biostimulators.

You may see the product written as HILOWAVE, Hilowave or HILO WAVE. These are the same injectable product. If a treatment called HILO WAVE is described as needle-free, it is a different procedure from the HILOWAVE injectable used at Klinik Dr Diana.

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>HILOWAVE is a doctor-performed hyaluronic acid biostimulator for skin ageing, with gradual, variable results and a still-developing evidence base.</strong> It suits people whose main concern is skin quality and early ageing, not people looking for lifting or new facial volume.</p>
</div>

## What is inside HILOWAVE?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>HILOWAVE contains three forms of hyaluronic acid: non-cross-linked high-molecular-weight (large-molecule) HA, non-cross-linked low-molecular-weight (small-molecule) HA, and a minimally cross-linked combination of large- and small-molecule HA.</strong> Each form is designed to play a different role in the skin.</p>
</div>

Hyaluronic acid (HA) is a sugar molecule found naturally in the skin. It binds water and forms part of the supporting network around skin cells. Skin HA levels fall with age and sun damage.

## How does HILOWAVE work as a hyaluronic acid biostimulator?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>HILOWAVE is based on two ideas: HA variability and HA duration.</strong> Different sizes of HA do different jobs in the skin, and minimal cross-linking keeps HA in the skin for longer. Together, they are designed to modulate the skin's architecture and microenvironment so that it favours regeneration at the cellular level.</p>
</div>

The skin's microenvironment is the setting that skin cells live in: the water, HA, collagen and elastin around them. As this environment thins with age, skin cells such as fibroblasts become less active. The theory behind HA biostimulation is that loading the skin with HA of different sizes, and keeping it there longer, restores a better environment. This helps revitalise skin cells so that they renew the skin themselves.

This approach differs from collagen stimulators that rely on a controlled inflammatory reaction. In laboratory work, the HILOWAVE HA complex supported collagen production while lowering inflammatory signals. This mechanism currently rests on laboratory and animal studies, not human trials.

## What does the research on HILOWAVE show so far?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>The main published study on HILOWAVE is a 2026 preclinical study — laboratory cell experiments and a rat model — published in the International Journal of Molecular Sciences.</strong> As of October 2026, we have not found a peer-reviewed human clinical trial of HILOWAVE.</p>
</div>

The 2026 study tested HILOWAVE (listed in the paper as DHC, a dual hyaluronic acid compound) on human skin cells in the laboratory and in UV-damaged rat skin. It reported changes across all four signs of skin ageing, together with fewer inflammatory signals from immune cells. The authors describe their findings as showing biological potential rather than proven clinical results in people.

What the published research does not yet tell us:

<ul class="clean">
<li>How much visible improvement to expect in human facial skin</li>
<li>How long HILOWAVE results last in people</li>
<li>The ideal number of sessions and spacing, based on human trials</li>
<li>How HILOWAVE compares with other biostimulators in real patients</li>
<li>Whether a laboratory finding of reduced melanin in pigment cells means anything for melasma. HILOWAVE should not be considered a pigmentation treatment.</li>
</ul>

Klinik Dr Diana explains the evidence level of every treatment before you decide. HILOWAVE is a newer product, and its human evidence is still developing compared with longer-established biostimulators.

HILOWAVE may be considered for:

### Is HILOWAVE a filler?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>No. HILOWAVE is a biostimulator, not a filler.</strong> A filler is a firmer, more heavily cross-linked gel used to add defined volume or reshape areas such as the cheeks, chin or under-eye hollows. HILOWAVE is placed within the skin to improve skin quality and support regeneration.</p>
</div>

For more on how injectables placed within the skin differ from fillers, read <a href="/blog/skin-booster-vs-filler">skin booster vs filler</a>.

</div>

You may be suitable if:

<ul class="clean">

## What happens during a HILOWAVE session at Klinik Dr Diana?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>A HILOWAVE session usually takes about 30 to 45 minutes, including numbing time.</strong> It involves a skin assessment, cleansing, numbing cream, small injections within the skin, and aftercare advice before you leave.</p>
</div>

### 1. Consultation and skin assessment

The doctor reviews your concern, medical history, medicines, current skincare, and any previous injectables or laser treatments. The doctor then assesses the four ageing parameters — skin matrix, elasticity, skin activity and hydration — as well as texture and pigmentation. If HILOWAVE is not the right fit, the doctor will say so and explain the alternatives.

### 2. Treatment plan and consent

The treatment areas, amount of product and injection technique are planned. You are told the expected course, possible side effects and the cost before you agree to proceed.

### 3. Numbing and injection

Numbing cream is applied for about 15 to 20 minutes, then the skin is cleansed with antiseptic. HILOWAVE is injected in small amounts within the skin using a fine needle, a cannula or both, depending on the area. Most people feel brief stinging or pressure.

### 4. Aftercare and review

Small bumps at the injection points are expected and usually settle within a few days. You receive written aftercare advice and a review date if the doctor recommends one.

## When will you see results from HILOWAVE, and how long do they last?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>Some people notice their skin looks more hydrated within one to two weeks of HILOWAVE, once swelling and bumps settle; changes in elasticity, texture and firmness, if they occur, develop more gradually as the skin responds.</strong> There is no published human data yet on how long HILOWAVE results last; in current practice, maintenance is usually reviewed every 3 to 6 months after the initial course.</p>
</div>

How your skin responds to HILOWAVE depends on your age, skin condition, sun exposure, smoking, skincare routine, hormonal changes and previous treatments. Results vary depending on skin type and severity of the concern, and individual assessment is required. HILOWAVE should not be expected to produce the effect of a filler, a lift or a resurfacing laser.

## How do the doctors at Klinik Dr Diana decide whether HILOWAVE is the right option?

<div class="answer">
<p><strong>At Klinik Dr Diana, the doctor first identifies what is making the skin look aged or tired — loss of matrix, elasticity, cell activity or hydration, or another cause such as pigmentation, scarring or volume loss — and only then chooses a treatment.</strong> HILOWAVE is considered when the main problem is skin ageing at the quality level, and it may be combined with other treatments when more than one concern is present.</p>
</div>

The order of treatment matters. A typical decision path looks like this:

<ol>
<li><strong>Active acne is treated first.</strong> Injecting into inflamed skin raises the risk of infection.</li>
<li><strong>Pigmentation or melasma is brought under control with its own plan.</strong> HILOWAVE may be added later for skin quality.</li>
<li><strong>Acne scars need scar-specific treatment</strong>, such as subcision, TCA CROSS or fractional laser. HILOWAVE may support overall skin quality alongside it.</li>
<li><strong>Volume loss or sagging needs a structural option</strong>, such as a polymer or CaHA collagen stimulator, HIFU or filler. HILOWAVE alone will not lift the face.</li>
<li><strong>When the main issue is loss of hydration, elasticity, texture or skin quality</strong>, HILOWAVE is considered.</li>
</ol>
