## What Is Profhilo Structura?

Profhilo Structura is an injectable treatment made from highly purified hyaluronic acid, designed to work in the deeper layers of the face to support firmness and structure. It belongs to a group of treatments called "bio-remodelling" injectables, which encourage your own tissue to repair and strengthen. [1]

It is a denser, higher-concentration version within the Profhilo family, made specifically to be placed deeper, in the facial fat compartment layer, rather than just under the skin. [1] In simple terms, while the original Profhilo improves skin quality near the surface, Structura works deeper to help support areas that have lost firmness.

---

## How Does Profhilo Structura Work?

Profhilo Structura works by being placed into the deeper fatty layer of the face, where it helps support the fat compartments and encourages the tissue to remodel and firm up over time. [1] It is made from a stabilised hyaluronic acid complex that contains no chemical cross-linking agents, and it gradually stimulates the skin's own repair processes.

In a pilot study, it was used to help restore the lateral cheek, or outer cheek, fat compartment, an area that commonly loses support with age. [1] Because it works through gradual remodelling rather than instant filling, the result is meant to look natural and softly supported rather than obviously augmented.

## Is Profhilo Structura a Filler?

No, not in the usual sense. A traditional filler is placed to add volume or define a specific contour, while Profhilo Structura spreads to support and remodel the deeper tissue, aiming for firmness rather than shape change. [1]

This difference matters for natural results. Many Asian patients want to look fresher and firmer without looking "done" or over-projected, and a bio-remodelling approach is designed around supporting the face rather than reshaping it.

## Why Does Asian Facial Ageing Need a Different Approach?

Asian faces often age differently from the typical Western pattern, so the treatment plan should reflect that. For many Asian individuals, the first and most noticeable sign of ageing is soft-tissue sagging and loss of support, rather than fine surface wrinkles. [2]

There are anatomical reasons for this. Asian faces tend to have a flatter, less projected midface and rely more on soft tissue and fat for support, and the deep fat compartments of the midface gradually thin with age. [3] When this deeper support weakens, the cheeks can lose projection and the face can look like it is descending or deflating. This is sometimes called "pseudoptosis," where volume loss creates the look of sagging. [2,3] This is why an approach built around deeper support and firmness, rather than just smoothing lines, often fits Asian ageing better.

## How Is the Profhilo Structura Technique Adapted for Asian Faces?

The technique is adapted by focusing on where Asian faces actually lose support, and by aiming for natural firmness rather than added projection. The starting point is a careful assessment of the face, looking at which deep fat compartments have deflated and where the tissue has started to descend. [4]

In practice, this means planning treatment to support areas of genuine deflation and descent, while being conservative in a midface that is naturally broad or flat, so the result looks refreshed rather than wide or over-filled. [2,4] Because the goal is support and remodelling, the approach is gentle and gradual, and it is always individualised. There is no single "one technique fits all," which is why this kind of treatment should be planned and performed by a trained doctor after a proper assessment, not from a fixed template.

## What Concerns Can Profhilo Structura Help With?

## What Are the Realistic Expectations?

Realistic expectations are important: Profhilo Structura is a support and remodelling treatment, not a facelift. It may help the face look softly firmer and more supported, but it does not produce the dramatic lift of surgery, and results vary from person to person. [1,2]

It also works gradually, so improvements build over weeks rather than appearing immediately, and maintenance is needed over time. If the main concern is significant, advanced sagging, an honest assessment may suggest that other approaches are more suitable.

---

## How Many Sessions Are Needed, and What About Downtime?

Profhilo Structura usually starts with two sessions about a month apart, followed by maintenance over time, with results building gradually. [1] The improvement comes from the tissue remodelling itself, so it is not instant.

Because it is injected deeper, it may cause mild swelling or bruising, which usually settles within a few days. Most people return to normal activities quickly, and following aftercare advice helps recovery.

---

## Is Profhilo Structura Safe? Who Should Avoid It?

Profhilo Structura is generally well tolerated, as it is made from a substance similar to what the body already contains, but it is not suitable for everyone and should be performed by a trained medical practitioner. [1] As with any injection, temporary redness, swelling, or bruising can occur, and deeper injections carry the usual injection-related risks, which careful technique and assessment help reduce.

It is usually avoided or needs extra caution in people who are pregnant or breastfeeding, have an active skin infection or inflammation at the treatment area, or have a known allergy to the product. A full medical history and assessment should always come first.

## Is Profhilo Structura Suitable for Asian Skin?

Yes, it is generally considered suitable for Asian skin. Because it is hyaluronic-acid-based and does not rely on heat, it carries a low risk of the pigment problems, or dark marks, that can sometimes follow heat-based treatments in Asian skin.

As always, "low risk" is not "no risk," and good technique and aftercare still matter. The bigger factor for Asian faces is planning the treatment around the right anatomy and goals, which is why assessment is so important.

## What Are the Risks of Choosing the Wrong Treatment?

The main risk is mismatching the treatment to the problem, for example, expecting a bio-remodelling treatment to lift heavy sagging that really needs another approach, or over-treating a naturally broad midface so it looks wider rather than refreshed. [2] Matching the treatment to the actual change is the point of assessment.

Other considerations include temporary swelling or bruising, the usual risks of any injection, and unrealistic expectations of a surgical-level lift. A clinic that assesses first and explains the limits honestly helps you avoid these problems.
