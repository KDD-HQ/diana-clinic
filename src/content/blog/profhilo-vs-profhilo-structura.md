Profhilo and Profhilo Structura are both injectable bio-remodelling treatments made from stabilised hyaluronic acid, but they serve different purposes. Profhilo focuses on deep hydration, skin texture improvement, and elasticity — making the skin look smoother and more radiant. Profhilo Structura provides structural support, lifting sagging areas and restoring lost volume in the midface and jawline. For aging skin that has lost both quality and support, combining both treatments typically delivers the most comprehensive results.

## How Does Profhilo Work and Who Is It For?

Profhilo is a bio-remodelling treatment that stimulates your skin's natural production of collagen and elastin. Unlike dermal fillers, it does not add volume or change facial contours. Instead, it hydrates the skin from within, improves texture, and enhances overall skin quality.

Profhilo is administered using the BAP (Bio Aesthetic Points) technique — five injection points on each side of the face, each delivering 0.2 ml into the superficial subcutaneous layer. This covers the malar and submalar regions while avoiding major vessels and nerves.

Choose Profhilo if your primary concern is skin quality — dullness, dryness, fine lines, and loss of that natural glow. Results typically last around 6 months, though patients with mature or very dry skin may benefit from sessions every 3–4 months.

## How Does Profhilo Structura Work Differently?

Profhilo Structura is designed for deeper structural support. While it uses the same stabilised hyaluronic acid technology, its formulation targets sagging tissues and volume loss rather than surface hydration. It works by stimulating collagen in the deeper tissue layers and has been shown to support adipocyte (fat cell) viability — helping maintain the natural volume that gives the face its youthful fullness.

Profhilo Structura is administered using a cannula with retrograde injection technique for precise, deeper placement. It targets the superficial fat compartments, making it ideal for lifting and contouring the cheeks, jawline, and nasolabial fold area.

Choose Profhilo Structura if your primary concern is tissue descent, sagging, or volume loss. Results can last up to 9 months, with maintenance recommended every 6–9 months.

## When Should You Combine Both?

For comprehensive anti-aging, combining Profhilo and Profhilo Structura addresses both surface quality and structural support simultaneously. Profhilo ensures the skin looks hydrated, smooth, and radiant, while Structura provides the lift and contour definition that reverses sagging.

| Feature | Profhilo | Profhilo Structura |
|---|---|---|
| Primary goal | Hydration, texture, glow | Lift, volume, contour |
| Target depth | Superficial subcutaneous | Deep fat compartments |
| Technique | BAP points (needle) | Cannula, retrograde |
| Duration | ~6 months | Up to 9 months |
| Best for | Dull, dry, fine-lined skin | Sagging, volume loss |

Results for both treatments vary between individuals. Neither treatment is permanent, and maintenance sessions are recommended for sustained results. An in-person assessment is essential to determine which treatment or combination is most appropriate for your specific concerns.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Profhilo the same as a dermal filler?**
No. Profhilo does not add volume or reshape facial contours. It works by stimulating your skin's own collagen and elastin production, improving quality from within. Fillers physically fill spaces and change volume — Profhilo remodels the skin itself.

**How many sessions are needed?**
Profhilo typically requires 2 initial sessions spaced 4 weeks apart, then maintenance every 4–6 months. Profhilo Structura follows a similar protocol with longer intervals between maintenance sessions.

**Is there any downtime?**
Both treatments involve minimal downtime. Small bumps at injection sites typically resolve within 24 hours. Most patients return to daily activities immediately after treatment.
