## What Are Profhilo and Profhilo Structura?

Profhilo and Profhilo Structura are injectable treatments made from highly purified hyaluronic acid, a substance that naturally holds water and supports the skin. They belong to a group of treatments called bio-remodelling injectables, which means they encourage your own skin and tissue to repair and strengthen.

Unlike traditional fillers, they are not designed to fill a line or change facial shape. Instead, they spread within the tissue and stimulate the skin to improve itself. Profhilo is the original product, and Profhilo Structura is a newer member of the same family designed to work deeper.

---

## How Are They Different From a Normal Filler?

The key difference is that these treatments improve and support your tissue rather than adding shape or volume the way a normal filler does. A traditional filler is placed to plump a specific area or define a contour, while Profhilo and Profhilo Structura spread more widely to improve skin quality and firmness.

In simple terms, a filler fills, while these treatments remodel. This is why they tend to give a more natural, refreshed look rather than an obvious change in shape, which many patients prefer.

---

## What Does Profhilo Do?

Profhilo mainly improves the quality of the skin - its hydration, firmness, smoothness, and glow. It works more superficially, spreading within the skin and stimulating collagen and elastin, the proteins that keep skin firm and elastic.

It is often chosen by people who feel their skin looks dull, dry, or has started to lose firmness, but who do not want their facial shape changed. The result is gradual and natural, improving the skin's overall condition rather than altering features.

---

## What Does Profhilo Structura Do?

Profhilo Structura works deeper, helping to support the facial fat compartments and restore firmness in areas that have started to sag or lose volume with age. It is a denser formulation designed to be placed in the deeper fatty layer of the face.

As we age, the face gradually loses deep fat and support, which can make it look less firm or slightly hollow. Profhilo Structura aims to support these deeper layers so the face looks softly firmer and more supported, without the obvious filled look of a shaping filler.

In a pilot study, it was used to help restore the lateral cheek, which is the side of the cheek area.

---

## What Is the Main Difference Between Profhilo and Profhilo Structura?

The main difference is depth and purpose: Profhilo improves skin quality more superficially, while Profhilo Structura works deeper to support facial structure and firmness.

Their formulations also differ. Profhilo Structura contains a higher concentration of hyaluronic acid, about 90 mg per 2 ml, compared with the original Profhilo, which contains about 64 mg per 2 ml. This gives Profhilo Structura a denser consistency suited to deeper placement.

Both are made with the same hybrid hyaluronic acid technology and contain no chemical cross-linking agents. So they are partners rather than rivals - one focuses on the skin's surface quality, the other on deeper support.

## Which One Do I Need?

It depends on your main concern. If your skin looks dull, dry, or is losing firmness but your facial shape is fine, Profhilo may suit you. If your main concern is deeper sagging or a loss of firmness and support, especially as part of natural ageing, Profhilo Structura may be more relevant.

In practice, many people, particularly from around their 40s onward, have a mix of both skin-quality and deeper-support concerns. This is exactly why an assessment is more useful than guessing. A doctor can look at which layer is driving the change you want to improve.

## Can Profhilo and Profhilo Structura Be Used Together?

Yes, they can often be combined because they work at different depths and complement each other. One improves skin quality, while the other supports deeper firmness.

Used together, they aim for a natural, refreshed, softly supported result rather than an obvious change.

Whether to combine them, and in what order, depends on your concern, your skin, and the doctor's plan. Combining treatments is common in aesthetic medicine, but it should be planned individually rather than added automatically.

---

## How Many Sessions Are Needed, and What About Downtime?

Both treatments usually start with two sessions about a month apart, followed by maintenance over time, with results that build gradually. The improvement is not instant, because the treatments work by encouraging your own tissue to remodel.

Downtime is usually short. Profhilo may leave small temporary bumps at the injection points that settle within a day or so, while Profhilo Structura, being injected deeper, may cause mild swelling or bruising.

Most people return to normal activities quickly, and following aftercare advice helps recovery.

---

## Are Profhilo and Profhilo Structura Safe?

Both are generally well tolerated, as they are made from a substance similar to what the body already contains, but they are not suitable for everyone and should be done by a trained medical practitioner.

As with any injection, side effects such as redness, swelling, small bumps, or bruising can occur and are usually temporary.

These treatments are usually avoided or need extra caution in people who are pregnant or breastfeeding, have an active skin infection or inflammation at the treatment area, or have a known allergy to the product. A full medical history and assessment should always come first.

---

## Are They Suitable for Asian Skin?

Yes, they are generally considered suitable for Asian skin. Because they are hyaluronic-acid-based and do not rely on aggressive heat, they carry a low risk of the pigment problems or dark marks that can sometimes follow heat-based treatments in Asian skin.

As always, low risk is not no risk. Good technique, suitable planning, and proper aftercare still matter, and an assessment helps confirm the treatment fits your skin and concern.
