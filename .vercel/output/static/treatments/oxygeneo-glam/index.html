What is OxyGeneo Glam Facial?

OxyGeneo Glam is a 3-in-1 medical facial that combines gentle exfoliation, CO₂-induced internal oxygenation (the Bohr effect), and infusion of 24K gold flakes, Carnosine Peptide, Silk Amino Acids, Hyaluronic Acid, and PhytoSpherix™. Unlike traditional oxygen facials that spray oxygen onto the skin externally, OxyGeneo triggers the skin’s own physiological oxygen response from within — producing visible radiance with cumulative anti-aging benefits.

At Klinik Dr Diana, OxyGeneo Glam is our flagship glow-and-firming protocol — selected because it delivers visible results from the first session without irritation, peeling, or required recovery time. Treatments are performed under doctor supervision at our Bandar Baru Bangi (Selangor) and Senawang (Negeri Sembilan) branches.

How does OxyGeneo Glam work?

OxyGeneo Glam works in three sequential stages: exfoliation, oxygenation, and active infusion. The signature mechanism is CO₂-induced oxygenation, which triggers the Bohr effect — a physiological response where elevated local CO₂ causes hemoglobin to release more oxygen into the treated tissue.

1. Exfoliation

An OxyGeneo OxyPod reacts with a Primer Gel applied to the skin, gently dissolving the stratum corneum (outer dead skin layer). This is non-abrasive — unlike microdermabrasion — and prepares the skin to absorb actives more efficiently.

2. CO₂ Oxygenation (Bohr Effect)

The OxyPod-gel reaction generates a CO₂-rich bubbling layer at the skin surface. The body interprets this as a localised oxygen deficit and responds by directing oxygen-rich blood to the area. The result is improved microcirculation, enhanced cellular metabolism, and a visible flush of healthy radiance.

3. Active Infusion

While the skin is primed and oxygenated, the device delivers ultrasound-assisted infusion of 24K gold flakes, Carnosine Peptide, Silk Amino Acids, Hyaluronic Acid, and PhytoSpherix™. Gold supports antioxidant and luminosity benefits; Carnosine Peptide signals collagen and elastin pathways; Silk Amino Acids and Hyaluronic Acid strengthen the skin barrier and improve hydration retention.

What is inside the Glam OxyPod?

The OxyGeneo Glam OxyPod contains five clinically active components: 24K Gold Flakes for antioxidant and luminosity benefit, Carnosine Peptide to signal collagen and elastin pathways, Silk Amino Acids to repair the skin barrier, Hyaluronic Acid for deep hydration, and PhytoSpherix™ for moisture retention and skin-glycoprotein support.

  • 24K Gold Flakes — antioxidant, anti-inflammatory, enhances surface light reflection for radiance.
  • Carnosine Peptide — a dipeptide that signals fibroblasts toward collagen and elastin synthesis; primary mechanism for the anti-aging benefit.
  • Silk Amino Acids — small molecules that penetrate the stratum corneum to improve hydration retention and barrier strength.
  • Hyaluronic Acid — humectant that binds water in the dermis; contributes to the immediate plumping and smoothing effect.
  • PhytoSpherix™ — plant-derived glycogen that supports skin hydration, energy metabolism, and barrier function.

Who is OxyGeneo Glam suitable for?

OxyGeneo Glam is suitable for adults seeking visible glow, hydration, and early anti-aging support without required downtime. It is commonly chosen for pre-event skin preparation, dehydrated or dull skin, and patients who cannot tolerate the recovery period of more invasive procedures.

  • Early to moderate signs of aging (fine lines, loss of firmness, dullness)
  • Dehydrated skin or compromised glow
  • Pre-event skin prep — weddings, photography, public appearances
  • Patients seeking gentle, non-invasive options
  • Maintenance between laser or injectable sessions

Not suitable for: active skin infections, severe inflammatory dermatoses, open wounds, or a significantly compromised skin barrier. Suitability is assessed by our doctors during in-person consultation.

What to expect during the session

A full session at Klinik Dr Diana takes approximately 45 minutes. After cleansing, the OxyGeneo OxyPod and Primer Gel are applied — you’ll feel gentle bubbling and a light warming sensation. The device is then moved over the face in circular motions during the oxygenation phase, followed by the active infusion. Most patients describe the experience as relaxing rather than uncomfortable.

There is no peeling, no scabbing, and no required downtime. Makeup can be applied immediately afterward. For maintenance, we typically recommend treatments every 3 to 4 weeks.

Possible side effects

OxyGeneo Glam is generally well tolerated. Possible short-term effects include mild transient warmth and light flushing of the skin for approximately 10 to 30 minutes after the session. Temporary skin sensitivity is uncommon but can occur in patients with reactive skin. There is no peeling, scabbing, or required recovery period.

Individual results vary depending on skin type, baseline condition, age, and lifestyle factors. An in-person consultation is required to confirm suitability and design a personalised treatment plan. This page is for general educational information and does not constitute medical advice.

Frequently asked questions

How fast will I see results?

Most patients see visible improvement after the first session — particularly glow, hydration, and smoother makeup application. Anti-aging benefits build over multiple sessions spaced 3 to 4 weeks apart. Individual results vary.

Is the treatment painful?

No. Most patients describe OxyGeneo Glam as a relaxing massage-like experience, with mild warmth or tingling during the oxygenation phase.

How often should I do OxyGeneo Glam?

For maintenance, every 3 to 4 weeks is typical. For pre-event preparation, a single session 24 to 72 hours before the event is usually sufficient. Your doctor will confirm the right interval during consultation.

Can I combine OxyGeneo Glam with other treatments?

Yes. It pairs well with pico laser (for pigmentation), skin boosters such as Skinvive or Rejuran, and injectables. Our doctors will design a combination plan during consultation.

Is OxyGeneo Glam safe for sensitive skin?

It is non-abrasive and well tolerated by most skin types in many cases. We avoid it in active skin infection, severe inflammatory skin disease, or a compromised barrier. Suitability is confirmed in person.
